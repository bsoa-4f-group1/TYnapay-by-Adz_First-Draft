History & Establishment

The Pandemic Inception — April 2020
TYnapay by Adz was established during the COVID-19 lockdowns in the Philippines. Husband-and-wife team Adrienne Ty, also known as Adz, and Eric Ty began baking at home when their real estate work was affected by the pandemic. Baking became a therapeutic outlet and an alternative source of income.

The Brand Name and Identity
The name is a wordplay on the Tagalog word tinapay. The letters TY recognize the family surname and express “Thank You” to God and their supporting community. The brand follows the philosophy of making products “with so much love.”

The First Product and Early Growth
The business began with an order of one dozen Ube Cheese Pandesals for a neighbor. Through word of mouth, the menu expanded to include Classic Cheese Rolls, Cinnamon Rolls with Cream Cheese Glaze, and Coffee Buns.

Evolution into Event Catering — 2024 onward
The TYnapay + Kape Mobile Event Cart expanded the business into mobile food and beverage catering for corporate events, brand activations, and weddings. The draft lists clients and events involving The Peninsula Manila, Uratex Philippines, GCash, RCBC, DEEPAL, Omoda, and Jaecoo.

Founders: Adrienne Ty (“Adz”) and Eric Ty.
